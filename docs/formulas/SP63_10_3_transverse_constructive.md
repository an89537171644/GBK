# SP63 10.3 - transverse reinforcement constructive spacing draft

requires_engineer_review = true

## Scope

Draft-MVP constructive checks for beams and ribs with rectangular sections.

## Minimum Stirrup Diameter

For bending elements in tied reinforcement cages, the MVP minimum stirrup
diameter is 6 mm.

## Spacing When Transverse Reinforcement Is Required By Calculation

When transverse reinforcement is required by the shear check, the stirrup
spacing is limited to:

- not more than `0.5 * h0`;
- not more than `300 mm`.

## Spacing When Concrete Carries The Shear

When shear is carried by concrete in the draft MVP check, for beams and ribs
with height at least 150 mm, the stirrup spacing is limited to:

- not more than `0.75 * h0`;
- not more than `500 mm`.

## Not Implemented Yet

- support zones;
- punching;
- torsion;
- anchorage;
- reinforcement curtailment zones.
